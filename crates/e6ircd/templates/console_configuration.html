Configuration

Operational settings are versioned in PostgreSQL. The attach listener applies immediately; core identity and limits apply on restart.

Identity

Names and text advertised to IRC clients.

Storage retention

Bound durable chat history and privileged audit data. Message history retention applies to the server's channel history and to every network's bouncer history, direct messages included. Expired browser, API, device, and logout credentials and monitoring samples past their retention are cleaned by the same supervised maintenance cycle.

BNC attach listener

Enable the raw IRC endpoint clients use to attach to their always-on networks. Attaching clients send their account password, so any address other than loopback requires a TLS certificate.

IRC listeners

Raw, TLS, and dedicated WebSocket endpoints. Listener changes are checked now and applied after restart.

Web and administrator access

The HTTP bind and database remain bootstrap settings. Public identity, cookie policy, and administrator grants are UI-managed.

Capacity

Process and per-connection resource bounds. Blank optional limits are unlimited.

Monitoring history

Control the bounded PostgreSQL time series used by the live monitoring console. Changes apply immediately.

SASL requirement

Refuse IRC clients that have not logged in to an account by the end of registration, as a SASL-only network does. Bouncer sessions are exempt.

Account registration

Policy advertised through the IRC account-registration capability.

Server networks

Shared or account-owned networks imported from [[network]]. They start from the persisted configuration after restart.

IRC operators

Passwords are encrypted with the deployment master key and are never rendered back to the browser.

OpenID Connect

Identity providers used for browser sign-in. Client secrets are encrypted and replace-only.
