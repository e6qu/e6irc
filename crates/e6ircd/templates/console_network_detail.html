Client certificate

A TLS client certificate signs this network in without a password: with SASL EXTERNAL where the network offers it, and otherwise by its fingerprint, which NickServ's CertFP recognises (OFTC). Saving reconnects the network.

No client certificate is stored.

    NickServ account

    Sign in to this network with a NickServ account. Saving reconnects the network.

    Open IRC client

    The account and password are stored in this network's settings, which is the one place they are edited: open its settings. An empty password box there keeps the stored one.

    Register a new NickServ account

    Each step sends a standard message to NickServ. Its replies appear in the IRC transcript below, and the same commands work from chat with /msg NickServ ….

    Libera Chat refuses account creation from some hosting-provider addresses. If its reply says a verified account is required, do steps 1 and 2 from another IRC connection, then save the account and password in this network's settings.
    Libera SASL guide
    1

    Request the email

    /msg NickServ REGISTER <password> <email>

    2

    Verify the email

    /msg NickServ VERIFY REGISTER <nick> <code>

    3

    Save the account

    When NickServ confirms the verification in the transcript, enter the account and the password from step 1 in this network's settings and save; the network reconnects and signs in with them.
